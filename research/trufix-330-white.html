TRUFIX 330 WHITE

TRUBUILD TILE ADHESIVE TA 330 White is a single-component, ready-to-use, polymer modified, cement- based, white tile adhesive for fixing ceramic, clay, terracotta, brick, query tiles, vitrified/fully vitrified tiles, glass mosaic tiles, terrazzo, basalt, porcelain tiles & other stones on floors/walls in interiors, and exterior walls upto 10 ft height in dry and wet areas. It should be applied on a fully cured and sound cementitious substrate.

    FAQ

    What is Trufix 330 White?

    Trufix 330 White is a high-quality, polymer-modified, white cement-based tile adhesive designed for fixing light-colored or translucent tiles on interior and exterior floor and wall substrates. It provides excellent bonding strength, improved flexibility, and water resistance, along with a bright white color that enhances the appearance of the installation.

    What are the key features of Trufix 330 White?

    Trufix 330 White is a white-colored, thin-set, cement-based tile adhesive with enhanced performance characteristics, such as:

    • High bonding strength and shear resistance
    • Improved flexibility and crack-bridging ability
    • Good sag resistance for vertical applications
    • Extended open time for easier installation
    • Water resistance for wet area applications
    • Bright white color for light-colored or translucent tiles
    • Compatibility with a wide range of tile types and sizes

    What are the advantages of using a white cement tile adhesive like Trufix 330 White?

    White cement tile adhesives like Trufix 330 White offer several advantages, particularly for installing light-colored or translucent tiles, such as:

    • The white color prevents any darkening or shadowing effect behind the tiles
    • It enhances the brightness and aesthetics of glass, marble, or light-colored tiles
    • It provides a uniform and consistent substrate color for thin or translucent tiles
    • It eliminates the need for additional white underlayments or membranes
    • It ensures a seamless and professional finish for high-end installations

    What substrates can Trufix 330 White be used on?

    Trufix 330 White can be used on various stable, dry, or damp substrates, including concrete, cement screed, cement backer board, brick masonry, cement plaster, gypsum plasterboard, and existing ceramic tile or natural stone surfaces. It is suitable for both interior and exterior applications on floors and walls.

    What tile types can be fixed with Trufix 330 White?

    Trufix 330 White is compatible with a wide range of tiles, including ceramic, porcelain, vitrified, mosaic, glass, and natural stones like marble, granite, and limestone. It is particularly recommended for light-colored, translucent, or thin tiles that require a white background for optimal aesthetics.

    How is Trufix 330 White mixed and applied?

    Trufix 330 White is mixed with clean water in the ratio of 4-4.5 parts of powder to 1 part of water by volume to obtain a lump-free, creamy consistency. The adhesive is then spread on the substrate using a notched trowel, and the tiles are pressed firmly into the adhesive bed, ensuring complete coverage and a uniform thickness.

    What is the recommended notched trowel size for applying Trufix 330 White?

    The recommended notched trowel size for applying Trufix 330 White depends on the size of the tiles and the substrate condition. For tiles up to 300 mm x 300 mm, use a 6 mm x 6 mm square-notched trowel. For tiles larger than 300 mm x 300 mm or uneven substrates, use a 12 mm x 12 mm square-notched trowel to ensure adequate coverage and support.

    What is the open time and pot life of Trufix 330 White?

    Trufix 330 White has an open time of approximately 20-30 minutes at 30°C, which is the maximum time interval after application during which the tiles can be fixed without loss of adhesion. The pot life of the mixed adhesive is around 3 hours at 30°C. Do not add more water or use the adhesive beyond its pot life.

    How long does Trufix 330 White take to set and cure?

    Trufix 330 White sets in 24 hours and achieves full bond strength in 14 days at 30°C and 50% relative humidity. The tiles can be grouted after 24 hours, and the tiled area can be subjected to light foot traffic after 48 hours. Protect the tiled surface from water exposure and heavy loads during the curing period.

    Can Trufix 330 White be used for installing glass mosaics or translucent tiles?

    Yes, Trufix 330 White is highly recommended for installing glass mosaics, translucent tiles, or any light-colored tiles that require a white background for uniform appearance and brightness. Its white color, high bonding strength, and improved flexibility make it ideal for such delicate and aesthetically demanding installations.
