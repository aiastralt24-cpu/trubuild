PRODUCT DESCRIPTIONS
Trubuild IW+ 111 is silicate-based, integral, water prooﬁng compound which is specially designed to reduce porosity in
cement concrete, mortar & plaster. It helps in reducing permeability of concrete and makes it more cohesive.
Meets the requirement of IS: 2645-2003.


                                                      FEATURES & ADVANTAGES








                                                         IMPROVES                  BETTER                 REDUCES                ENHANCES
                                                         FINISHING                COHESION              PERMEABILTY            WORKABILITY

                                                         Reduces permeability in concrete, thereby improving the water tightness and
                                                         reduces the corrosion in reinforced steel.
                                                         Single component product, easy to use.
                                                         It improves workability of freshly mixed concrete and increases durability of
                                                         concrete without affecting the strength of concrete.

METHOD OF APPLICATION

Ÿ    Charge cement & aggregates to concrete mixer as per the mix design. Mix in dry state for 1–2 minutes. Start addition of
    75–80% mixing water & mix for 2-3 minutes.
Ÿ    Cure the applied mortar or concrete as per good construction practices.
Ÿ    Trubuild IW+ 111 is added as per the recommended dosage into the remaining mixing /gauging water, then add to concrete
    mixer & mix for another 2 minutes. Maintain the water to cement ratio as low as possible.
