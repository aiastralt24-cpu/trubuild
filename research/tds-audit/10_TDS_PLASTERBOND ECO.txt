              PLASTERBOND ECO

                        Bonding Agent for Plaster
                                                                                      TECHNICAL DATA SHEET VER NO. N.1

PRODUCT DESCRIPTIONS

TRUBUILD PLASTERBOND ECO has a unique hybrid polymer emulsion technology formula which provides excellent
bonding between RCC and gypsum plaster or sand cement based plaster.




                                                         FEATURES & ADVANTAGES








                                                           EXCELLENT              SINGLE COAT
                                                         BOND STRENGTH            APPLICATION

                                                             Excellent cohesive bond to RCC, concrete and other surfaces.
                                                             Better adhesion /bonding due hybrid polymer technology
                                                             Application in one coat
                                                             Blue in colour for easy identification





AREAS OF APPLICATION                                                       PACKAGING






                                                                                            20 kg





 • Brush application on RCC/ concrete
 • For gypsum plastering or sand cement-based plastering

METHOD OF APPLICATION

Clean all type of dirt, oil, grease, other agents and loose material from substrates like RCC before application. After opening
the container, mix the inside material well before using it. Apply a single brush coat on the substrate and leave it for 6-8 hours.
Then surface will be ready for gypsum plaster or any sand cement-based plastering.

HANDLING & STORAGE

HANDLING: Mandatory and recommended industrial hygiene procedures should be followed whenever the product is
being handled and used. For additional information, please read Material Safety Data Sheet.

STORAGE: Product should be stored in a dry place in its original, properly closed containers at temperature between 20°C -
40°C. Chemical should be consumed once opened.
