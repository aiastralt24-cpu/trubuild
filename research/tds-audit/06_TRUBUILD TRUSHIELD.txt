                         TRUSHIELD


          Preformed SBS Membrane for Basement Waterproofing

TRUBUILD TRUSHIELD is a high-quality, SBS-modiﬁed, self-adhesive, cold-applied sheet waterprooﬁng membrane. It has
a cross-laminated HDPE ﬁlm on one side and a silicon release ﬁlm on the other. It provides excellent waterprooﬁng and high
endurance, making it ideal for below-ground structures.

METHOD OF APPLICATION
Surface Preparation
Ensure that surfaces are thoroughly cleaned of all contaminants such as dust, curing compounds, oil, and grease. All surface
imperfections,  protrusions,  structurally  unsound,  and  friable  concrete  must  be  removed  and  repaired.  Fill  surface
irregularities such as blowholes, honeycombs, etc., with a suitable concrete repair mortar.

Priming
Apply Trubuild Trushield Primer @ 4–6 sq. m/litre using a brush or roller over the prepared substrate, as per the recommended
coverage. Lay the membrane once the primer reaches a touch-dry state. Prime only an area that can be covered within the
same working day.

Application
Unroll the membrane and align it according to proper markings across the area. Re-roll the membrane into its original form to
maintain alignment.
Peel oﬀ the release ﬁlm from the self-adhesive side and press the membrane onto the surface. Smoothen it from the center
toward the edges using a wooden press to remove any entrapped air.
Use an iron roller over the applied membrane to ensure proper and strong adhesion of the bitumen compound with the base
surface.
Ensure a minimum side overlap of 50 mm and an end overlap of 100 mm.

Protection
The Trushield SBS membrane must be protected immediately after application from damage due to ongoing site activity or
sharp aggregates during backﬁlling. Use a tough, weather-resistant, warp- and rot-proof asphaltic protection board.
Alternatively, on horizontal areas, protection can also be provided by laying a 50 mm cement-sand screed.

PRECAUTIONS
Ÿ    Do not apply during rain or extreme temperatures.
Ÿ    Trushield membranes are not UV- or weather-resistant and must be protected immediately after application.
Ÿ    Avoid mechanical abuse that could puncture the membrane.
Ÿ    Ensure the membrane is applied at least 6 inches inside the drain pipe.
Ÿ    For optimal performance, apply the membrane parapet to parapet to envelope the entire structure.
Ÿ    Low temperatures may reduce adhesion and tack, while high temperatures may soften the material and make it overly
     adhesive.
