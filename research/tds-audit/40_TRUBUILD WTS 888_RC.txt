PRODUCT DESCRIPTIONS
TRUBUILD WTS 888 (Water Thinnable Sealer) is a single-component, water thinnable, polymeric liquid for sealing porosity
of sand stone, concrete and other porous surfaces. Water can be added to any quantity depending upon porosity of the
surface and gloss required.
