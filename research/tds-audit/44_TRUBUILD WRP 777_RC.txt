PRODUCT DESCRIPTIONS
Trubuild WRP 777 (Water Repellent) is silane-based, non-flammable, water soluble solution designed to incorporate water
repellence to varied surface like sand stone, marble, concrete, exposed bricks and terracotta tiles. It is used for waterproofing
of most of the construction surfaces as it penetrates deeply in the surface and provides excellent water repellence without
changing the appearance/colour of substrate.
Meets the requirement of IS: 12027: 1987.

METHOD OF APPLICATION
Ÿ    Surface to be treated should be dry and free from all dirt, oil, debris and all other contamination which could prevent
    penetration.
Ÿ    Apply one coat of Trubuild WRP 777 by brush and leave the same to dry for next 24 hrs.
Ÿ    Apply second coat wet on wet to improve the penetration and performance and again leave the same to dry for the next
    24 hrs.


RESTRICTIONS ON USE
Ÿ    Trubuild WRP 777 should not be diluted further as it will reduce its effectiveness.

HANDLING
Mandatory and recommended industrial hygiene procedures should be followed whenever our products are being handled

STORAGE
Product should be stored in a dry place in its original, properly closed container at a temperature between 2-40°C. Partially
emptied containers should be closed immediately.
