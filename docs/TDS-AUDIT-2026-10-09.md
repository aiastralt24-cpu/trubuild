## Conflicts within supplied documents

These are internal transcription observations, not public warnings or approval blockers; original PDFs were not altered. Ambiguous/placeholder table entries are not represented as validated specifications.

- **Aquapoxy:** The supplied TDS states negative water pressure of 0.5–1 bar in its benefits and up to 3.5 bar in its table. It also gives 8–10 hours between tank coats versus 6–8 hours in the table. Confirm project pressure limits and recoating intervals with technical support.
- **Trushield:** The supplied TDS leaves the roll-dimension unit unfinished and the tensile-strength unit ambiguous. Those entries are not treated as validated specifications; confirm them with technical support. The packaging section states a 1 m × 10 m roll.
- **Crackfill Powder:** The supplied TDS has an unrelated waterproof-coating subtitle. Its application section states 3–5 mm cracks, while its restriction says less than 5 mm. Confirm suitability at exactly 5 mm.
- **SBR 333:** The supplied TDS bonding-primer composition table contains a missing quantity, and some test-standard identifiers appear incomplete. Use the stated 1:1 volume ratio in the application instructions and obtain clarification for specifications.
- **TRU PU:** The supplied TDS gives 6–8 hours between coats in the method and 24 hours in the limitations. Confirm the recoating interval before application. The approximately 24-hour cure value is not permission for immersion; ponding tests require ten days.
- **Crystaseal 1K:** The supplied TDS gives conflicting tank-filling rates (feet per hour versus metres per 24 hours). Obtain a confirmed filling schedule from technical support.
- **Crystaseal Admix:** The supplied TDS contains unfilled fresh/hardened concrete table rows and differs on alkali content (<9% in benefits versus <4% in the table). Placeholder rows are omitted; confirm the specification with technical support.
- **Tru PU Pura 1K:** The water-pressure test standard is printed as EN 1239-8:2019 in the supplied TDS. Confirm the standard identifier before specification.
- **TCSR-555:** The supplied method states 1 kg/m² at 1 mm thickness, while its coverage section states 15–20 sq ft/kg for two coats at 300 microns. Confirm the required thickness and consumption for the proposed application.
- **WRP 777:** The supplied TDS says to dry the first coat for 24 hours and also apply the second coat wet-on-wet. Confirm the intended recoating method before use.
