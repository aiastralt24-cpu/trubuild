## Final prompt set

Architecture: Photorealistic architectural editorial photograph for a premium construction waterproofing website. A sculptural modern Indian residence, pale concrete cantilever with an opening framing cobalt blue sky, terracotta wall, limestone courtyard and reflecting pool edge. Low courtyard perspective, hard afternoon light, plausible detailing, natural matte textures. No people, text, logos or packaging. Illustrative concept, not a documented project.

Interior: Photorealistic contemporary bathroom with large beige limestone tiles, warm teak bench, subtle black fixtures, courtyard doorway and tropical leaves. Side lighting, visible grout lines, plausible squared detailing, editorial architectural photography. No people, text or packaging. Illustrative application concept.

Logo cleanup: Recreate the supplied TRUBUILD wordmark on a solid dark charcoal #19211f background. Preserve original letter shapes and proportions. TRU in yellow #ffca05, BUILD in white, all letters fully filled and opaque. Remove photograph, foliage and rain; retain small white TM. No extra design or new logo. (An initial transparent extraction had artifacts and was rejected.)

Yellow concept revision: Retain the original full-page structure, section order and typography. Replace orange brand accents with logo yellow #ffca05, use charcoal header and footer, supplied yellow/white wordmark, yellow buttons with charcoal text, yellow Advisor band with dark CTA, white content areas. Keep architectural imagery, solution bento, featured product area and footer. No gradients or rounded corners. Dark text replaces yellow-on-white headings in implementation for accessible contrast.

## Intentional implementation adaptations

Authentic product photography replaces neutral packaging silhouettes in the concept. Real product descriptions, source notes, technical tables and additional required company/resources pages extend the concept. Generated architecture is presented with an explicit concept caption. User-selected yellow is carried by controls, text highlights, section bands and the logo; natural terracotta tones remain in the architectural image.

Roof: A broad pale concrete rooftop terrace on a contemporary Ahmedabad building, low parapet, a concrete canopy, distant city skyline and blue sky. Matte surface with plausible fall, no puddles, no people, products or branding. Illustrative architecture. Saved as public/images/roof.webp.
